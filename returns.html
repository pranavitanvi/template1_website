Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be entirely satisfied with your Aura purchase. If for any reason you are not, we accept returns and exchanges within 14 days of delivery.

1

Initiate Request

Contact our concierge team at concierge@aura.com or call us within 14 days of receiving your item to request a return authorization.

2

Secure Packaging

Place the unworn item back in its original presentation box, along with the certificate of authenticity and all original packaging.

3

Complimentary Pickup

We will arrange a secure, fully-insured courier pickup from your location at no additional cost to you.

*Please note: Custom or engraved pieces are final sale and cannot be returned.
