Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer custom designs?
Yes, we offer bespoke design services. You can collaborate with our master designers to create a truly one-of-a-kind piece. Please book a consultation to begin the process.
Are your diamonds ethically sourced?
Absolutely. We adhere strictly to the Kimberley Process and only source diamonds from trusted suppliers who guarantee conflict-free stones.
Can I upgrade my diamond later?
Yes, we offer a lifetime diamond upgrade program for solitaire rings purchased from Aura. You can upgrade to a larger stone by paying the price difference.
Do you provide certificates of authenticity?
Every purchase includes an Aura Certificate of Authenticity. Diamonds over 0.50 carats are accompanied by a grading report from internationally recognized laboratories like GIA or IGI.
